Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application The Dumbest Guard: Bouncer (the "Application"), developed and provided as a free-to-play mobile game.

Key Summary: The Application does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location).

1. Information Collection and Storage

All gameplay data (such as high scores, unlocked streak records, and audio preferences) is stored exclusively on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application may use third-party services that collect anonymous device-level identifiers to provide core app functionality and in-game advertisements. You can review the privacy policies of these providers below:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

3. Device Permissions

The Application requires minimal device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, or personal files. The vibration permission (VIBRATE) is used solely to provide haptic feedback when tapping in-game buttons and can be disabled in the game settings at any time.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

5. Security

We value your trust. Because the Application does not transmit personal data across the internet, your personal information is not exposed to remote data breaches.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at: support.thedumbestguard@gmail.com
